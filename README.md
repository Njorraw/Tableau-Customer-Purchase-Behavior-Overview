🛍️ Customer Purchase Behavior Overview


📊 Project Summary

This Tableau dashboard provides a detailed view of customer purchasing patterns based on **age**, **gender**, **product category**, and **purchase frequency**. It's designed to help marketing and sales teams uncover actionable insights and tailor campaigns to high-value segments.

---

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset (`Customer_Purchases.csv`) includes the following fields:

- `Customer ID`
- `Name`
- `Gender`
- `Age`
- `Purchase Date`
- `Product Category`
- `Product`
- `Quantity`
- `Unit Price`
- `Total Spend`

---

🎯 Objectives

- Track monthly customer spend trends.
- Identify top product categories by revenue.
- Segment customers by age group and gender.
- Evaluate purchase behavior across demographics.

---

📊 Visualizations

1. **Line Chart** – Monthly total spend.
2. **Bar Chart** – Spend by age group.
3. **Pie Chart** – Spend by product category.
4. **Heatmap** – Product categories vs. customer IDs (spend).
5. **Scatter Plot** – Age vs. Total Spend per customer.

---

🧩 Dashboard Filters

- Gender  
- Age Group  
- Product Category  
- Date Range  

---

🧠 Key KPIs Displayed

- 💰 **Total Spend**
- 👤 **Most Frequent Buyer**
- 🛒 **Top Spending Category**
